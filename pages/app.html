The church app

The house,
in your pocket.

TwainMan Ministry is a free app for the congregation. It carries the same teaching, the same calendar and the same prayer list that the hall does — into the rest of your week.

What is inside

Seven things.

  • Sermons and study
  • Discipleship courses
  • Events, added to your calendar
  • Prayer requests
  • Community groups
  • Giving
  • Announcements

Teaching & study

The sermon library and the courses.

Sermons can be listened to with the screen off, saved for later and picked up where you left them. Discipleship courses run in lessons, with study notes and short quizzes.

Recordings from the 2024 Gethsemane Camp Meeting were being edited for the library.

Prayer

Send a request.

Prayer requests go to the prayer ministry through the app. You can send one for yourself or on behalf of someone else.

Community & giving

Groups, announcements, and giving.

Find a group that meets near you, read the church's announcements, and give — all from the same place.
