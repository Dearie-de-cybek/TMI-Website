Who we are

One ministry,
two doors.

There is the house on OKP Road — a hall in Asaba where the congregation meets, sings and is taught. And there is the TwainMan Ministry app, which carries the same teaching, the same calendar and the same prayer list into the week.

About the church→

What happens here

Teaching, prayer and a monthly consecration of the work of our hands.

The church's own flyer says it plainly: every second Sunday, at eight in the morning, the congregation brings the work of its hands to be blessed.
